NEM Lens

DER in the NEM

Household batteries and rooftop solar in Australia's National Electricity Market, by region and month. Built from the Clean Energy Regulator's small-scale installation data and AEMO's rooftop PV estimates.

    Period
    Regions

    Home batteries

    CER has battery capacity by postcode only from July 2025, when home batteries began earning certificates under the Cheaper Home Batteries Program. Before that there is only a voluntary count, which CER says under-counts.

    Rooftop solar

    Installed capacity is CER's: systems up to 100 kW. Output is AEMO's half-hourly estimate per region, using its MEASUREMENT estimate where there is one and SATELLITE otherwise.

    Data and code: github.com/nem-lens/der. Every chart's table is a CSV in data/. Recent months are incomplete: CER counts a system once its certificates are created, up to a year after installation.

    Battery and solar figures are based on Clean Energy Regulator material licensed under CC BY 4.0, aggregated from postcode to state, region and month.
